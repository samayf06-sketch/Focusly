Privacy Policy

Focusly: Smart Task & Deadline Manager. Last updated: October 2026

Focusly helps students keep track of assignments and deadlines. This page explains what the app stores and what it does with it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, and your password (stored only as a one-way BCrypt hash, never in readable form).
  • Your tasks: the titles, descriptions, subjects, due dates, priorities, hours and statuses that you type in.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or device identifiers. The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to log you in, show your tasks, rank them, and remind you about deadlines. We use your email address only to log you in. We do not send you emails, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone.

Where it is stored

On the server that runs the app, in its database. Login sessions use a random token; only a fingerprint of the token is stored. Reminders and alarm times that you set on your device are kept on the device.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: in the app go to Settings → Privacy & data → Delete my account. Your account and all your tasks are permanently erased.
  • Reminders: alarms at 9:00 AM and 8:00 PM are built in. You can stop pop-up notifications in your browser or phone settings at any time.

Children

The app is meant for students. It does not knowingly collect more information from children than the account details above, and a parent or guardian may ask us to delete an account.
